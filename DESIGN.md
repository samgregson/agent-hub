# Agent Hub design direction

A simple, contemporary workspace for engineers collaborating with an agent. High-contrast black and white surfaces, clear sans-serif typography and restrained accents keep attention on the work. Use uppercase, tracked labels for primary navigation, sentence case for content, and black for the strongest actions. Keep grey to dividers and supporting text. Use consistent button heights, borders, padding, focus states and disabled states across the workspace. Keep navigation quiet, lists compact and the conversation central. Preserve keyboard access, responsive layouts and approval flows. MCP Apps receive the standard light theme.

The current Mott MacDonald website and public identity examples inform the emphasis on whitespace, clear typography and consistency across applications. Adapt those principles to a functional engineering workspace, while retaining Agent Hub's own identity. References: https://www.fredboucher.co.uk/mottmacdonald and https://medicineman.agency/portfolio/mott-macdonald/.

The site’s crisp contrast, simple geometry and confident hierarchy guide the interface; use compact application controls and avoid oversized marketing layouts. Reference: https://www.mottmac.com/.

Reviewed the current home, projects, markets and services, and insights pages. The repeated treatment is a black-and-white palette, large sans-serif headlines, uppercase primary navigation, spacious image-led sections and selective black action emphasis. Adapt the navigation and action hierarchy to the denser workspace.
